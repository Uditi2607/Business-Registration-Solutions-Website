What Is GeM Seller Registration?

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is the official online procurement portal launched by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. It facilitates online procurement of common use goods and services required by various Central and State Government departments, Ministries, PSUs, and autonomous bodies.

Registering as a seller on GeM gives your business direct access to government tenders, direct purchase orders, and L1 bidding opportunities. Whether you are an Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), an authorized reseller, or a service provider, GeM opens doors to a massive public sector market.

Document Checklist

Documents Required for GeM Registration

Keep these details ready for primary profile creation and vendor assessment on the GeM portal.

Business Identity

  • PAN Card of Business / Proprietor
  • Aadhaar Card of Authorized Signatory (linked with mobile)
  • GSTIN Certificate (Active GST Status)
  • Incorporation Certificate / Partnership Deed (if applicable)

Financial & Tax Proofs

  • Active Bank Account Details & Cancelled Cheque
  • Income Tax Return (ITR) filings for last 1 to 3 financial years
  • Turnover details verified by CA (where required)
  • Registered Business Address Proof

Product & Brand Details

  • Trademark Certificate / Brand Authorization Letter (for OEMs)
  • Product Images, Specifications, and Model Numbers
  • MSME / Udyam Certificate (for EMD & Fee waivers)
  • BIS / FSSAI / ISO Quality Certificates (if category requires)

Note: Primary registration on the GeM portal is free of government charge. GeM requires sellers to deposit Caution Money based on annual turnover thresholds. Final document requirements will be confirmed during consultation.
