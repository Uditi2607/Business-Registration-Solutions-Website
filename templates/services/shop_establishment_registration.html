Understand the registration

What Is Shop & Establishment Registration?

This is a state-level registration or licence for businesses such as shops, offices and commercial establishments. It helps establish compliance with the applicable state Shop and Establishment Act, including rules that may cover working conditions, records, hours and employment.

The issuing authority, process, documentation, certificate validity, renewal and government fees vary between states and may also depend on the business type. Our team helps identify the applicable local requirement before filing.
