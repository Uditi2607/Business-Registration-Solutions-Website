What Is IEC Registration?

An Import Export Code (IEC) is a unique business identification issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry. It is generally required for businesses that import goods into India or export goods and services from India.

IEC helps customs, banks and trade systems identify your business in international transactions. It is issued online through the DGFT portal, and one IEC is issued against a single PAN. The electronic IEC can be accessed through the DGFT dashboard.

An IEC normally has permanent validity unless it is suspended or cancelled by the competent authority. Keeping your IEC details current is important for smooth trade operations.

Documents & Details Required

The DGFT application is online. The exact details depend on your entity; we confirm the final checklist before filing.

Proprietorship

  • Proprietor and business PAN details
  • Applicant identity and contact details
  • Business address proof; Aadhaar may support a proprietor address
  • Cancelled cheque or bank certificate for the firm account
  • GST details, where applicable

Partnership Firm

  • Firm PAN and business address details
  • Partnership deed where needed to support entity details
  • Authorised signatory and contact information
  • Cancelled cheque or prescribed bank certificate
  • GST details, where applicable

Company / LLP

  • Entity PAN and registered address details
  • Certificate of Incorporation or LLP details where applicable
  • Authorised signatory information and digital signing method, as applicable
  • Cancelled cheque or bank certificate in the entity name
  • GST details, where applicable

DGFT requires address proof and bank proof with the IEC application. Ensure the account-holder name matches the firm name and all uploaded details are accurate.
