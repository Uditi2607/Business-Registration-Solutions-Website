Overview of Indian Passport Services

An Indian Passport is an official national travel document issued by the Consular, Passport & Visa (CPV) Division of the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India. It certifies the holder's nationality, citizenship, and identity for international travel.

Passport operations in India are conducted through Passport Seva Kendras (PSK) and Post Office Passport Seva Kendras (POPSK). Our team provides complete advisory, document pre-audit, online application filing, fee payment, and appointment slot booking at your nearest PSK/POPSK.

Applicant Categories

Who Can Apply for an Indian Passport?

Any Indian citizen by birth, descent, or registration is eligible to apply for a passport.

Adult Citizens

Indian citizens aged 18 and above (issued 10-year validity passports under ECR or Non-ECR category).

Minors (Below 18 Years)

Children requiring passports for international education, family travel, or sports (Annexure D required).

Non-ECR Category (Emigration Check Not Required)

Applicants with Class 10/Matriculation or higher education qualifications, income tax payers, or senior citizens.

Urgent / Tatkal Applicants

Travelers needing urgent passports due to employment, medical emergencies, or overseas admission.

Document Checklist

Documents Required for Passport Applications

Keep these mandatory supporting documents ready for your Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) appointment.

Proof of Present Address

  • Aadhaar Card (matching present address)
  • Active Bank Account Passbook with photo & stamp
  • Electricity / Water / Gas Connection Bill (recent)
  • Registered Rent Agreement / Employer Certificate

Proof of Date of Birth

  • Aadhaar Card / Birth Certificate
  • Class 10 / Matriculation Passing Certificate
  • PAN Card / Driving License / Service Photo ID
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from recognized school

Non-ECR & Specific Proofs

  • Class 10 / Higher Education Pass Certificate (for Non-ECR)
  • Marriage Certificate / Joint Affidavit (for spouse name addition)
  • Old Original Passport (for renewal/reissue)
  • Annexure D / Parent Passports (for minor applications)

Note: Requirements, fees and procedures may vary depending upon your State, Union Territory or issuing authority. Our experts verify the latest applicable requirements before submission.

Troubleshooting

Common Passport Problems & Our Solutions

We resolve frequent documentation hurdles faced by passport applicants.

Accidental ECR Category Printing

Problem: Applicant qualifies for Non-ECR (Emigration Check Not Required) but fails to attach Class 10 certificate, resulting in ECR stamp.

Solution: We audit educational proofs before filing to ensure your passport is issued under the Non-ECR category.

Address Mismatch During Police Verification

Problem: Police report marked adverse because applicant has lived at present address for less than 1 year without providing previous address proof.

Solution: We structure your address history correctly in the application form to ensure smooth police verification.

Spouse Name Mismatch

Problem: Spouse name on marriage certificate differs slightly from spouse's passport spelling.

Solution: We guide on legal joint affidavits or marriage certificate endorsement to prevent PSK counter hold.

PSK Appointment Slot Unavailability

Problem: PSK appointment slots show full for several weeks in major cities.

Solution: We monitor slot openings dynamically to secure the earliest available appointment at your nearest PSK or POPSK.

Expert Advice

Tips for Faster Passport Approval

Follow these essential guidelines for a smooth PSK appointment and police verification.

Carry Original Documents

Always bring original hard copies of Aadhaar, PAN, and Passbooks to your PSK appointment along with self-attested photocopies.

Current Address Proof

Ensure your present address proof is active and updated; passbook entries must reflect recent transactions.

Inform Police Station

Visit your local Police Station promptly when notified for police verification to avoid report delays.

Check Pre-1 Year Stay

If you resided at multiple addresses in the past 1 year, disclose all addresses clearly in the application.

Normal applications are processed with standard police verification prior to passport issuance (taking 10–30 days). Tatkal applications prioritize appointment booking and passport printing with post-police verification (taking 3–7 days).

Non-ECR (Emigration Check Not Required) means you do not need emigration clearance when traveling abroad for work to 18 specified countries. Applicants who have passed Class 10 automatically qualify for Non-ECR status.

You can apply for passport renewal up to 1 year before its expiry date. Most international countries require your passport to have at least 6 months of validity remaining for visa issuance.

For minors under 18 years, requirements include Birth Certificate/Aadhaar, parents' original passports (if available), present address proof, and Annexure D signed by both parents.

If your passport is lost or stolen, immediately file an FIR at your local police station. Then apply for passport reissue under the "Lost/Damaged" category along with the FIR copy and Annexure F.

If passport renewal is applied before expiry without any change in address, police verification may be waived or conducted post-issuance. If there is a change in address, police verification will be conducted.
