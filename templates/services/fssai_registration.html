What Is FSSAI Registration?

FSSAI is the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. Every Food Business Operator (FBO) is required to obtain an FSSAI Registration or License for the food premises as applicable under food-safety law.

Basic Registration is for eligible petty food businesses; State and Central Licenses apply to other businesses based on their type, turnover, capacity, location and operations. The right certificate demonstrates that your business is working within India’s food-safety framework.

FSSAI compliance supports safe handling, preparation, storage, distribution and sale of food—building confidence among customers, partners and online platforms.

Documents Required

FoSCoS requirements vary by your license category, state and food-business activity. We confirm the precise checklist before filing.

Identity & Business Details

  • Applicant identity proof and photograph
  • Business constitution proof, where applicable
  • Business name, contact and food-category details
  • Authorisation details for an authorised signatory, where applicable

Premise Information

  • Business-premise address proof
  • Ownership proof, rent agreement or relevant NOC, as applicable
  • Layout or plan details where required for the activity
  • Water analysis or equipment details where applicable

Food-Safety Information

  • List of food products or categories handled
  • Food Safety Management System plan, where applicable
  • Technical-person details for applicable manufacturing activities
  • Activity-specific declarations or NOCs, where required

Final documents and eligibility are determined through the official FoSCoS Kind of Business criteria for the specific premise.
