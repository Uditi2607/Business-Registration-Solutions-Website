Understand Udyam

What Is MSME (Udyam) Registration?

MSME means Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise. Udyam Registration is the Government of India’s official online registration process for enterprises that meet the MSME classification criteria.

In simple terms, MSME is the business category and Udyam Registration is the official identity for that business. It provides a permanent Udyam Registration Number and online certificate, helping eligible enterprises access government programmes, finance and growth opportunities.

The process was introduced to make recognition easier through a single, paperless, self-declaration-based system. Registration has no renewal requirement.

What to keep ready

Information Required for Udyam Registration

The official process is paperless and self-declaration based—no documents or proofs are uploaded. Keep the following correct details ready for verification.

Proprietorship

  • Proprietor’s Aadhaar number and name, for Aadhaar verification
  • Proprietor’s PAN details
  • GSTIN details, where applicable under GST law
  • Business name, address, bank and activity information

Partnership Firm

  • Managing partner’s Aadhaar number and name
  • Firm PAN details
  • GSTIN details, where applicable under GST law
  • Business, partner and activity information for self-declaration

Private Limited Company / LLP

  • Organisation PAN and GSTIN, where applicable
  • Authorised signatory’s Aadhaar for verification
  • Company / LLP identity and address details
  • Business activity, investment and turnover information as required

The Government’s Udyam Registration itself is free. Business information is self-declared and PAN/GST-linked details are drawn from government databases as applicable.
