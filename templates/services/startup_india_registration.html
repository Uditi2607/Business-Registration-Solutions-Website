What Is Startup India Registration?

Startup India is a flagship initiative of the Government of India designed to build a strong ecosystem for nurturing innovation, empowering entrepreneurs, and driving sustainable economic growth.

Under this scheme, eligible entities (Private Limited Companies, Registered Partnerships, and LLPs) can apply for official DPIIT Recognition. Once recognized, startups gain access to tax holidays, easy compliance self-certification, fund-of-funds access, and intellectual property incentives.

Understanding DPIIT Recognition

The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) certificate is the official credential that qualifies an entity as a recognized startup in India.

Official Gateway

Serves as proof of official status for government schemes, private incubators, and investors across India.

Regulatory Relief

Allows self-certification under 9 environmental and labor laws, significantly lowering compliance burdens.

Capital & Funding Access

Unlocks eligibility for Government Seed Fund schemes, SIDBI Fund of Funds, and Angel Tax exemptions.

Who Qualifies

Eligibility Criteria for Startup India

To qualify for DPIIT recognition, your enterprise must fulfill the following mandatory government criteria.

Eligible Entity Type

Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or Limited Liability Partnership (LLP).

Entity Age (Up to 10 Years)

The entity must be within 10 years from its official date of incorporation or registration.

Turnover Cap (Under ₹100 Cr)

Annual turnover must not have exceeded ₹100 Crore in any financial year since incorporation.

Innovation & Improvement

Working towards innovation, development, or improvement of products, processes, or scalable services.

Original Business

Must be a new venture, not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business entity.

High Wealth & Job Creation

Has potential for scalable business models with high potential of employment generation or wealth creation.

Document Checklist

Documents Required for Startup India Registration

Keep these documents ready for a seamless DPIIT recognition filing process.

Entity Legal Proof

  • Certificate of Incorporation (Pvt Ltd / LLP) or Partnership Registration
  • Memorandum of Association (MOA) & Articles of Association (AOA) / Deed
  • Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card of the Entity
  • GSTIN (if registered under GST)

Promoter & Director Details

  • PAN & Aadhaar Card of Directors / Partners
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Official Email Address & Contact Number
  • Director Identification Number (DIN / DPIN)

Innovation Write-Up & Demo

  • Pitch Deck or Brief Write-Up explaining product innovation
  • Details of how your product/service solves a market problem
  • Website Link, Mobile App Link, or Video Demo
  • Patents, Trademarks, or Industry Awards (if applicable)
