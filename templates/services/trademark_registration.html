What Is Trademark Registration?

A Trademark is a unique visual symbol, brand name, wordmark, logo, slogan, or label used by a business to distinguish its products or services from competitors. In India, trademarks are registered under the Trademarks Act, 1999 by the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trademarks (CGPDTM).

Once you file a trademark application, you can immediately start using the **™** symbol next to your brand. After final approval and registration certificate issuance, you earn the legal right to use the prestigious **®** symbol, giving you 10 years of exclusive nationwide brand ownership (renewable indefinitely).

Document Checklist

Documents Required for Trademark Registration

Required documentation depends on applicant entity type. Requirements may vary depending upon applicant category or government authority.

Individuals / Proprietors

  • PAN Card and Aadhaar Card of Applicant
  • Logo or Brand Name Artwork (JPEG/PNG format)
  • Signed Power of Attorney (Form TM-48)
  • User Affidavit (if claiming prior usage date)

Startups & MSMEs

  • Udyam (MSME) Certificate or DPIIT Recognition Certificate (for 50% Govt fee concession)
  • PAN Card of Entity & Directors/Partners
  • Incorporation Certificate / Partnership Deed
  • Logo / Wordmark image file

Private Ltd / LLPs / Others

  • Certificate of Incorporation & Entity PAN
  • Board Resolution authorizing signatory
  • Signed Form TM-48 (Authorization)
  • Brand Logo & Product/Service Details

Note: Final document requirements will be confirmed during consultation based on your specified trademark class (Nice Classification 1 to 45).

Common Questions

Trademark Registration FAQs

The **™** symbol can be used immediately after filing your trademark application (Form TM-A). The **®** symbol can only be used after the Trademark Registry grants the official Registration Certificate.

Trademarks are classified into 45 categories under the international Nice Classification (Classes 1 to 34 for goods; Classes 35 to 45 for services). Your brand is protected in the specific classes under which it is registered.

Application filing and ™ symbol usage start within 24 hours. Complete registration and ® certificate issuance typically takes 6 to 12 months, depending on government examination and opposition periods.

The government filing fee per class is ₹4,500 for Individuals, Proprietorships, Startups, and MSMEs. For Companies, LLPs, and Large Firms without MSME certificate, the government fee is ₹9,000 per class.

If the examiner raises an objection under Section 9 (descriptive/generic) or Section 11 (similar existing marks), a formal written response must be submitted within 30 days. Our legal team handles examination report replies efficiently.

A registered trademark is valid for 10 years from the date of application filing. It can be renewed indefinitely every 10 years by paying the renewal fee.

Trademarks are territorial. An Indian trademark protects your brand within India. However, you can use your Indian application as a basis to apply in over 120 countries via the Madrid Protocol.
