What Is Copyright Registration?

Copyright registration grants exclusive legal rights to the creator or owner of an original work under the Copyright Act, 1957. It protects creative expression across literary works, software algorithms, source code, artistic designs, website layout, sound recordings, and video media.

While copyright exists automatically upon creation, official registration with the Copyright Office (DPIIT) provides prima facie evidence of legal ownership in courts of law. It acts as an unbreakable barrier against unauthorized copying, piracy, code theft, and intellectual property infringement.

Document Checklist

Documents Required for Copyright Registration

Specific document requirements vary by work category (Software, Literary, Artistic, Sound Recording, etc.).

Software & Source Code

  • Source Code & Object Code samples (PDF format, first & last 20 pages)
  • Software User Manual / Functional Specifications document
  • Applicant PAN & Aadhaar Card
  • NOC from Author/Developer (if owned by Company/Employer)

Literary & Written Works

  • 2 Original Copies of the written work/manuscript
  • Author Identity & Address Proof
  • Title and Language details of the work
  • Signed Power of Attorney / Filing Authorization

Artistic & Media Works

  • 2 Copies of the artwork / photo / design
  • Search Certificate (CC-1) from Trademark Registry (if work is capable of being used as logo)
  • NOC from publisher/author if applicable
  • Incorporation certificate (if applicant is a Company/LLP)

Note: Requirements may vary depending upon applicant category or government authority. Final document requirements will be confirmed during consultation.
