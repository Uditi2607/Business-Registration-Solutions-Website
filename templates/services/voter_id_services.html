Overview of Voter ID (EPIC) Services

The Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC), commonly known as Voter ID card, is an official identity document issued by the Election Commission of India (ECI) to eligible Indian citizens. It serves as proof of citizenship, age, address, and democratic voting entitlement.

Whether you have turned 18 and want to register as a first-time voter, moved to a new constituency, or need to correct spelling errors in your existing voter card, our dedicated consultants assist you through the National Voters' Services Portal (NVSP) and State Election Commission procedures.

Applicant Criteria

Who Can Apply for Voter ID Services?

Citizens fulfilling ECI age and residence requirements can register or update their voter profile.

First-Time Voters (18+)

Indian citizens who have reached 18 years of age on or before the qualifying ECI cutoff date.

Relocated Residents

Individuals shifting their permanent or current residence to a new house or new constituency.

Overseas Citizens (NRIs)

Indian passport holders residing abroad for employment or education who haven't acquired foreign citizenship.

Cardholders with Errors

Existing voter cardholders needing corrections in name spelling, photo clarity, or relative details.

Document Checklist

Documents Required for Voter ID Services

Ensure clear scanned copies of identity, age, and address proofs are submitted.

Proof of Age / DoB

  • Aadhaar Card / Indian Passport
  • PAN Card / Driving License
  • Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Body
  • Class 10 Marksheet / School Leaving Certificate

Proof of Ordinary Residence

  • Aadhaar Card / Indian Passport
  • Water / Electricity / Gas Bill (recent 3 months)
  • Bank Passbook with photo & address stamp
  • Registered Rent Agreement / Property Tax Receipt

Personal & Existing EPIC Proofs

  • Recent Passport-Size Photograph (White Background)
  • Existing Voter ID (EPIC) Number & Card Copy (for Form 8/Replacement)
  • Family Member's EPIC Number (for booth mapping)
  • Police FIR copy (for lost duplicate card requests)

Note: Requirements, fees and procedures may vary depending upon your State, Union Territory or issuing authority. Our experts verify the latest applicable requirements before submission.

Troubleshooting

Common Voter ID Problems & Our Solutions

We help resolve common delays and rejection issues faced by applicants.

Delayed BLO Verification

Problem: Application remains pending at "Field Verified by BLO" status for weeks without progress.

Solution: We help you locate your assigned Booth Level Officer (BLO) and Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) details for status escalation.

Name Misspelling in Voter List

Problem: Name or father's name spelled incorrectly in English or vernacular regional language on the electoral roll.

Solution: We file Form 8 for correction of entries with exact transliteration to ensure 100% legal accuracy.

Duplicate Registration Rejection

Problem: Application rejected because applicant already holds an EPIC card in their previous hometown.

Solution: We file a simultaneous transfer request under Form 8 to safely migrate your voter record without duplication.

Physical Card Undelivered

Problem: Application accepted online, but physical EPIC card not received via Speed Post.

Solution: We guide you in downloading your official e-EPIC PDF and applying for a replacement print card.
