National Identity

Overview of Aadhaar Services

Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identity number issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) on behalf of the Government of India. It serves as nationwide proof of identity and address for Indian residents.

Keeping your Aadhaar details—especially your mobile number, address, and biometrics—accurate and updated is essential for accessing government welfare subsidies, opening bank accounts, obtaining SIM cards, filing income tax returns, and executing property registrations.

Applicant Categories

Who Can Apply for Aadhaar Services?

UIDAI services are available for residents, minors, and non-resident Indians (NRIs).

Indian Residents

Any individual residing in India for 182 days or more in the preceding 12 months.

Minors (Baal Aadhaar)

Children below 5 years (blue Baal Aadhaar linked with parent's Aadhaar; biometrics not captured until age 5).

Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)

NRIs holding a valid Indian Passport can apply for Aadhaar upon arrival without waiting 182 days.

Existing Cardholders

Anyone needing updates due to marriage, relocation, phone change, or biometric changes.

Document Checklist

Documents Required for Aadhaar Updates

UIDAI accepts specific valid documents under Proof of Identity, Address, and Date of Birth.

Proof of Identity (PoI)

  • PAN Card / Voter ID Card / Driving License
  • Indian Passport / Service Photo ID Card
  • PDS / Ration Card with photo
  • Freedom Fighter Photo Card / Disability Card

Proof of Address (PoA)

  • Electricity / Water / Gas Bill (not older than 3 months)
  • Bank Account Passbook with photo & address stamp
  • Property Tax Receipt / Registered Rent Agreement
  • Head of Family (HoF) Aadhaar & Relationship Proof

Proof of Date of Birth (PoDB)

  • Official Birth Certificate issued by Registrar/Municipal Body
  • SSLC / Class 10 School Leaving Certificate
  • Indian Passport / Government Gazetted Officer Certificate
  • PENSION Payment Order (PPO)

Note: Requirements, fees and procedures may vary depending upon your State, Union Territory or issuing authority. Our experts verify the latest applicable requirements before submission.

Troubleshooting

Common Aadhaar Problems & Our Solutions

We resolve frequent obstacles faced during Aadhaar update applications.

Unlinked Mobile Number

Problem: Cannot receive OTP for online address changes or tax filing because old phone number is inactive.

Solution: We book an expedited slot at your nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra for mandatory biometric mobile link update.

PAN-Aadhaar Name / DoB Mismatch

Problem: Income tax return or bank KYC rejected due to spelling variations between PAN and Aadhaar.

Solution: We audit your supporting proofs and guide you on whether to update Aadhaar or PAN first for 100% data consistency.

Address Proof Upload Rejection

Problem: UIDAI rejects online address update due to document name mismatch or cropped utility bills.

Solution: We pre-verify document validity, image resolution, and seal/stamp clarity before portal upload.

Biometric Authentication Failure

Problem: Elderly citizens or manual laborers face fingerprint authentication failure at ration shops or banks.

Solution: We guide on Iris scan update and mandatory biometric refresh at official UIDAI centers.

Expert Advice

Tips for Faster Aadhaar Approval

Follow these essential guidelines to prevent application rejection by UIDAI examiners.

Keep Phone Active

Ensure your registered mobile number has active SMS service to receive verification OTPs.

Clear Color Scans

Always upload original color scans of documents rather than black-and-white photocopies.

Exact Name Match

Ensure your name spelling on address proof matches your existing Aadhaar name exactly.

Recent Bills Only

Utility bills (Electricity/Water/Gas) submitted as address proof must be less than 3 months old.
