Understand GST

What Is GST Registration?

GST registration is the process of obtaining a Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) from the government. This unique number allows a business to collect GST from customers, file tax returns and claim eligible input tax credit.

For businesses that meet the prescribed turnover threshold—or fall under mandatory registration categories such as interstate suppliers and many online sellers—GST registration is an important legal requirement. A valid GSTIN also gives customers, suppliers and government buyers greater confidence in your business.

Documents Required

Document requirements can vary by business activity and address. Our team will confirm the final checklist before filing.

Proprietorship

  • PAN Card and Aadhaar Card
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Mobile Number and Email Address
  • Cancelled Cheque / Bank Statement
  • Business Address Proof
  • Rent Agreement and NOC, where applicable

Partnership Firm

  • PAN Card of Firm
  • Partnership Deed
  • PAN & Aadhaar of Partners
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Business Address and Bank Account Proof
  • Authorisation Letter

Private Limited / LLP

  • PAN Card and Incorporation Certificate
  • MOA and AOA
  • PAN & Aadhaar of Directors
  • DIN and DSC, if applicable
  • Board Resolution and Address Proof
  • Bank Account Proof
